PANEL GRID
SYSTEM

Five families of page layouts for comics, collages, moodboards and carousel posts. Every layout follows one rule: each cut runs from edge to edge. That keeps every panel four-sided, so gutters stay even and slanted cuts stay clean.

  1. Cut edge to edge. Split a panel into rows or columns, never a T-junction across it.
  2. One gutter. The same gap between every panel, and around the inset.
  3. Ratios 0.6–1.6. No panel is more than about 2.5× its neighbour. Mosaic uses the golden ratio.
  4. 4–9 panels. At most one inset per page. Mirror any layout to get a new one.

GENERATOR

SHUFFLE

Builds a new page from the same rules: 2–3 cuts per level, alternating rows and columns, ratios from 0.6 to 1.5, a 45% chance of a small slant on each cut, and sometimes an inset. Each seed always gives the same page.

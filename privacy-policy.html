PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Package ID: app.palettez

Palettez is a free, open-source, local-first Android application designed for creators and designers. We collect nothing. Your creative work and device data belong strictly to you.

1. ZERO TRACKING, ZERO ANALYTICS, ZERO ADS

  • No accounts required: You never need an account, username, email, or password to use Palettez.
  • No third-party trackers: Palettez contains no tracking libraries, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics frameworks.
  • No crash diagnostic transmissions: Palettez does not transmit crash logs or telemetry to external servers.

2. PHOTOS AND COLORS STAY ON YOUR DEVICE

  • On-device processing: When you pick an image via the Android Photo Picker or take a photo with your camera, images are decoded and clustered strictly in device memory.
  • Zero photo uploads: Your images, camera captures, posters, and palettes are never uploaded or sent to any server.
  • Minimal permissions: Camera captures use the system camera app (TakePicture). Palettez declares zero CAMERA or storage permissions in AndroidManifest.xml.

3. NETWORK USAGE

  • Google Fonts only: Palettez connects exclusively via HTTPS (cleartextTrafficPermitted=false) to official Google Fonts hosts: fonts.google.com, fonts.googleapis.com, and fonts.gstatic.com.
  • No identifiers: Requests fetch the public font catalog and .ttf font files you choose to preview or download, carrying zero user or device identifiers.

4. LOCAL STORAGE & EXPORTS

  • Device preferences: Theme, default color count, and grid settings are stored locally with Jetpack DataStore.
  • Recent palettes & font cache: Saved locally inside your app sandbox; clearable anytime in Settings.
  • User-controlled exports: PNG, SVG, CSS, JSON, and .ttf files are saved only to locations you explicitly pick via the Android Storage Access Framework.

5. OPEN SOURCE & CONTACT

  • Apache 2.0 License: Palettez is open source so anyone can inspect and audit its privacy commitments.
  • Questions: Open an issue on our GitHub repository.
